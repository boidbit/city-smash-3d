Privacy Policy

City Smash 3D (including the Monster Dash mode) · Last updated: 19 October 2026

City Smash 3D is a casual game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not collect, sell or share personal information. This page explains exactly what the app does and doesn't do.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no tracking. We do not have a server that receives data from your device.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress (for example, which level you have unlocked, your star ratings and settings) in your device's local storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Permissions

The Android app only requests the Internet permission, which it needs to load game resources (see below). It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or location.

Third-party resources

To run, the game loads two things from outside providers when you open it:

  • Three.js (the 3D graphics library), served by cdnjs / Cloudflare.
  • The “Baloo 2” font, served by Google Fonts.

Like any website or app that loads files from a server, these providers can see standard connection information such as your IP address and device/browser type when your device downloads those files. We do not control this and receive none of it. See their own privacy policies for details: Cloudflare and Google.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children either. The game contains cartoon-style monster and helicopter action.

Playing in a browser

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add any cookies or trackers of our own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/boidbit/city-smash-3d/issues.
